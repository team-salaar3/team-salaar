About Team Salaar

United youth. Strong community. Positive change.

Team Salaar is a youth association created to bring young people together through friendship, service, leadership and community development.


We believe young people have the energy, ideas and passion to make a real difference. Our goal is to create opportunities for youth to learn, lead, help others and grow together.


Together, we can build a stronger, more responsible and better community for everyone.
